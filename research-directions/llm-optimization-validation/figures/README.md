# Editable study diagrams

The `.mmd` files describe the proposed research design. They are Mermaid sources, not measured results or implemented runtime components.

- [Study workflow](study-workflow.mmd): candidate generation, validation, decisions, and independent assessment.
- [Evidence separation](evidence-separation.mmd): development, optimization, confirmation, and protected audit evidence.
- [Pilot decision](pilot-decision.mmd): conditions for continuing or redirecting the method.

The documentation checker counts Mermaid source files; it does not render them or verify their scientific content. Three diagrams satisfy its structural convention and explain distinct parts of this proposal.
