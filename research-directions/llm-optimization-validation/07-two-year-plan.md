# Two-year plan

This is a relative 24-month planning allocation. A start date, budget, and institutional milestones have not been fixed. Publication turnaround and degree completion are not guaranteed by this schedule.

| Period | Work | Decision evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Reproduce a small task pool; document valid workloads; establish controlled timing and A/A checks | Environments are feasible and observations are repeatable |
| Weeks 3–4 | Record short optimization histories; compare conventional safeguards; independently classify failures | Evidence separates real violations from noise and permitted trade-offs |
| Weeks 5–6 | Investigate recurring mechanisms; trial a minimal selector only if justified | Continue/redirect decision against the strong combined baseline |
| Months 2–7 | Expand and analyze the empirical study; archive inputs, snapshots, protocols, and case evidence | Distinct findings and a reproducible study suitable for advisor/venue review |
| Months 8–14 | Develop and evaluate a focused method if the empirical result supports one | Practical advantage survives strong comparisons and component-removal tests |
| Months 15–19 | Evaluate frozen protocol on untouched tasks; broaden environment/configuration checks where justified | Generalization evidence and documented limits |
| Months 20–24 | Revisions, artifact cleanup, thesis integration, and examination preparation | Coherent contribution and departmental requirements addressed |

## Continue only with evidence

Continue if consequential failures occur across multiple projects after sound measurement and obvious safeguards, and if the task population supports a meaningful study. The method additionally requires a plausible cost/reliability advantage with useful optimizations retained.

Redirect early if most observations are invalid inputs, expected specialization, ordinary measurement noise, or accumulation fixed by checking the original baseline. If broad testing is inexpensive, a sophisticated selector may have little practical value.

Keep an empirical negative-result path, but evaluate its novelty and explanatory value. Do not promise three papers or treat the current questions as guaranteed independent publications. Review thesis sufficiency with the supervisor.

Dependencies include reliable measurement infrastructure, benchmark environment access, an affordable optimizer configuration, and statistical guidance. The plan does not require training a foundation model. Major environment reproduction delays should reduce initial scope transparently rather than consume the whole pilot unnoticed.
