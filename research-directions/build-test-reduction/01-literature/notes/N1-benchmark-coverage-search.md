## 3. Verdicts

**B1 (own asv suite executes none of the changed functions for many true optimizations; networkx 9/15 by profile, 4/15 by static reach): PARTIAL.**
Found: premise-level evidence that benchmark reach of changes is incomplete (Shahedi excludes commits with no targeting benchmark; Grambow reports 35-66% function-level suite coverage and a missed major change possibly due to no covering benchmark; FormulaCode lists mean workload coverage 41.24% and notes uncovered paths; SWE-fficiency drops PRs without a covering unit test).
Not found in our recorded searches: a measured per-commit rate, for Python/asv or any suite, of real performance-improving commits whose changed functions are executed by the project's own benchmark suite (with interval), or a profile-versus-static-reach comparison.
Safe wording: "Prior work excludes or notes benchmark-uncovered commits (Java/JMH, Go, Python asv-based benchmarks), but in our recorded searches we found no study reporting what fraction of real performance-improving commits have their changed functions executed by the project's own benchmark suite; we measure this for one project (networkx: 9/15 by profile, Wilson 36-80%)."

**C11 (full asv suite detects 2/10 injected frozen slowdowns; 5 of 7 misses have zero reaching benchmarks; dropping by reach/profile): PARTIAL for full-suite detection of injected slowdowns; NOT FOUND for Python/asv per-edit miss rates when dropping by reach/profile.**
Found: Laaber & Leitner 2018 (injected slowdowns, ABS 10-100%, Java/Go, abstract-only); Grambow (reduced suite missed 2 major changes); Laaber 2021/2022 on coverage-based prioritization (ordering, not dropping).
Not found: a Python miss rate for dropping benchmarks by reach or profile, or a split of full-suite misses by whether any benchmark reaches the edit.
Safe wording: "Prior microbenchmark work measures detection of injected or real regressions by full or reduced suites in Java and Go; in our recorded searches we found no Python/asv measurement of per-edit miss rates when benchmarks are dropped by reach or profile, nor a split of full-suite misses by whether any benchmark reaches the edit (our small sample: 2/10 detected, 5 of 7 misses unreached)."

## 4. Cautions
- Ding's 74-77% is a rate among tests that already cover the change; do not cite it as evidence about tests not covering the change. The paper conditions on impacted tests.
- FormulaCode and Laaber MSR 2018 must be read in full before citing numbers.
