Questions you are likely to ask (1 of 2)

Build is negligible and testmon solves tests. What is the contribution?

A measurement study whose lead finding is the oracle gap: 8 / 14 edits unreached, ≤ 4 / 14 visible at 1.2×, 1 / 10 slowdowns detected above the floor.

Is your cost headline an artefact of the suite’s setup?

Partly, and we measured it: ≈ 8× standard, 2.2–2.3× with setup reused. Still the largest part.

You say no AI. Who labelled the optimizations?

The rules have no AI. The labels come from two blind LLM raters plus an LLM adjudicator; κ = 0.948 is inflated. The rate moves by one edit across label sets (9 / 15 vs 8 / 14).

Historical commits are not an optimizer session.

Agreed; no session effect is claimed. Coverage and visibility are per edit, so they do not depend on it.
