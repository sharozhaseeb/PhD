Validating each optimization edit cheaply: what build, test and benchmark reduction can and cannot see

Reduced builds and targeted test and benchmark selection for iterative Python performance edits. Deterministic rules only, no AI in the method.

Sharoz Haseeb · Pilot on networkx and bottleneck · Paper draft: 14 pages

Open with the one-line problem: an optimizer makes many small edits, and each must be re-validated. We asked how much of that we can cut with deterministic reduction, and what the benchmark oracle can actually see. The headline: on networkx, the limiting part is the benchmark oracle, not build or test cost.
