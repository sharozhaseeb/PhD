RQ3 · visibility and detection
The three-factor framing is executed, visible (Amdahl-style share of case time), detectable (power against noise). The uncovered versus below-noise split itself is not new: Peass and Laaber and Leitner split misses this way. What we add is the measured rate on real optimization commits.
