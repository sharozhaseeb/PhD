# Conditional PhD development plan

## First four to six weeks: decide whether the idea deserves continuation

Week 1: complete closest-work checks; establish data availability and license; settle target and event ordering.
Week 2: reproduce a simple rolling/ACI baseline and a multi-step baseline; test label timing on hand-worked sequences.
Weeks 3-4: compare volatility scaling, daily-surprise probe, and same-feature conditional scale prediction on development data.
Weeks 5-6: run prespecified ablations and dependence-aware summaries on exploratory challenge data. Record a continue/reframe/stop decision.

This is a feasibility estimate, not a promised paper timeline. Stop if reproduction or data gates cannot be satisfied, or if the supposed mechanism is already captured by existing methods.

## Year 1: empirical foundation

Deliver a reproducible account of label maturity, information equality, and calibration response under controlled and observed transitions. A paper is justified only by substantive findings beyond existing backtesting work. Complete a systematic search with screening counts and archived inclusion/exclusion decisions.

## Year 2: method contribution if justified

Develop a compact correction responding to the established failure. Compare against stronger conditional uncertainty methods. Theory is optional pending mathematical feasibility: any result must state dependence, drift, boundedness, and delay assumptions. Do not promise universal conditional coverage.

## Year 3: transfer and synthesis

Validate in a genuinely untouched forward period and an independent instrument set or individual-stock universe with appropriate historical membership. Explain where the method fails and how much data it needs. Study a downstream decision only if this adds a separate contribution and the additional financial evaluation is feasible.

## Publication and degree planning

COPA and forecasting-methodology outlets provide topic precedent; that is not a measured acceptance probability. Agree acceptable venues and thesis expectations with the advisor. Three stages are not three automatic papers. A useful thesis must contain connected original contributions, not three variants of the same benchmark.

Repeat novelty checks before each new contribution. The reserve question about uncertain future covariates requires its own review and is not an automatic extension.
