# Decisions, risks, and stop criteria

The advisor has encouraged the researcher to choose an area of personal interest. Financial forecasting is the current preference; advisor publication history is not being used to force a domain change.

## Decisions before running a confirmatory study

1. Is the intended contribution applied forecasting methodology rather than stock-selection profitability?
2. Is a fixed equity-ETF pilot acceptable as a starting point before individual stocks?
3. Which data source permits the intended access and redistribution, and how are corporate-action vintages handled?
4. Which untouched periods and instruments can actually be sealed? Previously discussed crises are development or exploratory material.
5. Are the proposed effect-size and coverage guardrails meaningful for the thesis, and what sample length gives useful precision?
6. Which venues and contribution types satisfy the university's degree requirements?

No question blocks writing this proposal. Data licensing, uninspected test access, and the final statistical plan do block credible confirmatory claims.

## Findings from conceptual audit

The daily signal may simply be ordinary conditional volatility estimation. This is the largest novelty risk. AcMCP and multi-step PID make the earlier suggestion of a generic delayed-feedback contribution too broad.

A market regime is not known with certainty in real time. Operational rules may use only lagged or currently observed evidence. Retrospectively labeled crises may be reported as descriptive slices, not fed into the deployed policy.

A finite 90% interval on every day with arbitrary drift is not established. Reporting infinite intervals, local coverage uncertainty, and failure cases is necessary.

## Stop or reframe when

- An existing same-feature method matches the candidate.
- Improvement vanishes after purging overlapping targets or using issue-time normalization.
- A single event drives the pooled improvement.
- Score improvements fail the practical threshold or uncertainty spans no improvement.
- The effect requires hidden knowledge of regime onset.
- Dataset access prevents independent evaluation.

Do not respond to failure by expanding into agents, more models, more horizons, or repeatedly inspecting the holdout. A benchmark/negative-result route must independently earn its contribution.

## Reserve direction

Uncertainty in forecasted future covariates is explicitly discussed as future work by Wang and Hyndman. It is reserved for a separate review if the present pilot fails; it is not included to enlarge the current scope.
