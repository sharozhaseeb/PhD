## 2. Formula conventions (say them the way the code does)

- **ᾱ indexing.** In DDPM (Ho) t runs 1…T and ᾱ_t = ∏_{s=1}^{t} α_s. Our code computes ᾱ_t = ∏_{s=0}^{t} α_s with t = 0…999, so **code ᾱ_t = paper ᾱ_{t+1}**. For cosine, Nichol-Dhariwal define ᾱ_t = f(t)/f(0) with ᾱ_0 = 1. Our `alpha_bars[t]` = f(t+1)/f(0), recomputed from β clipped at 0.999, so ᾱ_999 ≈ 2.4e-9 and 1/√ᾱ_999 ≈ 20,000. The video's x_t / ᾱ_t labels must use 0…999 and must not show ᾱ_0 = 1.
- **β from the cosine schedule:** β_t = 1 − f(t+1)/f(t), clamp(max = 0.999), s = 0.008 (cell 10). The clamp is why cosine β_999 = 0.999 and 1/√α_999 ≈ 31.6.
- **Reverse step:** x_{t−1} = (1/√α_t)(x_t − β_t/√(1−ᾱ_t)·ε̂) + √β_t·z. Here ε̂ is the re-derived ε after clipping x̂_0. This is algebraically the posterior mean computed from the clipped x̂_0, which is why it matches "clip_denoised" in the original code.
- **LoRA scaling:** h = W_0x + **(α/r)**·B A x. PEFT uses α/r (rsLoRA α/√r is off). With α fixed at 16, **scale = 2 at r = 8 and 1 at r = 16**, so rank and scale are confounded. A is r×d_in, B is d_out×r, and B = 0 at init. Params per module = r(d_in + d_out).
- **Ω (Swin Eq. 1-2).** Ω(MSA) = 4hwC² + 2(hw)²C and Ω(W-MSA) = 4hwC² + 2M²hwC. The 4hwC² term is Q, K, V **and the output projection**. The 2 is QKᵀ plus the attention-weighted V. The formulas count multiply-adds and **ignore softmax**, relative-position bias, the mask and the number of heads. If the video shows a version "with softmax" (adding a term of order (hw)² or M²hw), it must label that as *not* the handout or report formula. **The numbers to say are 41,104,179,200 / 979,435,520 = 41.97.** SW-MSA costs the same as W-MSA (cyclic shift + mask).
- **IA3:** k′ = l_k ⊙ (W_k x), v′ = l_v ⊙ (W_v x), FFN: W_2(l_ff ⊙ GELU(W_1 x)). PEFT scales the **input** of a feedforward module (`output.dense`) and the **output** of k and v. All l start at 1.
- **NF4/double quant:** blockwise (block 64) absmax scaling onto 16 levels that are quantiles of N(0, 1). The levels are **not evenly spaced**, and 0 is represented exactly. Double quant re-quantises the fp32 absmax constants to 8 bit.

## 3. Diagram accuracy: what each diagram must NOT imply

- **DDPM:** no class-label input arrow (the model is unconditional). Do not draw training as running the chain: it is one random t per image and one U-Net call. Sampling = 1000 sequential calls. Do not draw noise added on the final step. Do not draw the cosine curve reaching ᾱ = 1 at t = 0 or exactly 0 at t = 999.
- **ViT:** the patch embedding is a single 16×16 stride-16 conv, applied once. After it there is **no downsampling and a constant 197×768**. The position embeddings are **learned** (not sinusoidal) and are added after [CLS] is prepended. Do not imply ImageNet normalisation.
- **Swin:** patch merging = concat 2×2 → 4C → LayerNorm → Linear to 2C. It is **not pooling**. A shifted window does not add windows (cyclic shift + mask). Blocks come in pairs, W-MSA then SW-MSA (depths 2, 2, 6, 2). Do not draw a shift in stage 4 of the 224 model.
- **Linear probe:** the backbones are frozen and in eval mode. The classifier is **sklearn**, not a PyTorch head, so draw no gradient flowing into the backbone. Swin features = GAP over the 7×7 grid; ViT features = [CLS] only. Augmented photos go to the train side only.
- **LoRA:** W_0 stays **frozen and unchanged** during training (no merge was done). The adapters sit on Q and V (plus FFN `output.dense` in 04 only), **not on K**, and not on every linear. Draw the classifier head as fully trained (not LoRA'd) and MuRIL's pooler as frozen.
- **QLoRA:** the weights are **stored** in 4 bit and **dequantised to fp16 for every matmul**; **nothing is computed in 4 bit**. The LoRA A/B, the LayerNorms, the embeddings and the classifier are **not** quantised (fp32). Gradients flow *through* the frozen 4-bit weights to the adapters, but the 4-bit weights get no update. The paged optimizer does **not** always live on the CPU: it pages out only under memory pressure, and here it never needed to, which is why 05 = 06.
- **NF4:** do not draw 16 evenly spaced levels or one scale for the whole tensor.
- **IA3:** three element-wise **vectors** (k, v, FFN-intermediate); there are no matrices. There is **no vector on Q**, and 07a has only k and v. The FFN vector sits on the 3072-d hidden activation, **before** `output.dense`, not on the 768-d output.
- **Tokenizer fertility:** show it as total tokens / total words, with specials excluded. Do not use the MuRIL fertility (1.313 < 1.401) to explain MuRIL's collapse. The causal link was not shown.
